03 — FEEDBACK / CONTROL

Core differentiator / vendor intelligence

The vendor feedback loop

1Requirement defines skills & locationRequirements
2Vendors are scored and rankedPure service
3Staff reviews fit and selects vendorsHuman action
4Submissions are deterministically screenedCandidateScreeningService
5Shortlist, interview, offer & hire outcomesWorkflow records
↶ Candidate quality and conversion outcomes update vendor performance / history, then feed the next ranking.

Decision boundary

People own the call.

Scores expose evidence; the AI assistant can draft a screening brief, contract review, feedback summary or job description. It must not make final hiring, rejection, compensation or offer decisions.

Vendor selection: HR / AdminShortlist and rejection: human reviewInterview feedback: panel inputOffer and approvals: authorized staff
